Digital Iqub Management System

A Django-based digital platform designed to modernize traditional Ethiopian Iqub savings groups by supporting group management, member registration, contributions, financial tracking, and digital payment workflows.

Project Overview

Digital Iqub aims to bring traditional community-based savings practices into a digital environment. The platform is designed to help organizers and members manage Iqub groups, monitor contributions, track financial activities, and access relevant information through a centralized system.

The project demonstrates my skills in Python, Django, database-driven web development, user authentication, role-based access control, and financial workflow management.

Main Features

- User Authentication: Secure user login and account management.
- Role-Based Access Control: Separate access for administrators, organizers, members, finance personnel, verification staff, and support personnel.
- Member Management: Register, manage, and organize Iqub members.
- Iqub Group Management: Support the management of daily, weekly, and monthly savings groups.
- Contribution Tracking: Record and monitor member contributions.
- Wallet Management: Support financial balance tracking and transaction records.
- Deposit Workflows: Integrate or prepare payment workflows for supported payment providers.
- Withdrawal Management: Manage withdrawal requests through an approval workflow.
- Draw Management: Support the management of Iqub draw activities.
- Transaction History: Maintain records of relevant financial activities.
- Notifications: Provide updates about contributions, transactions, group activities, and other events.
- Administrative Dashboard: Enable authorized personnel to oversee users, groups, and financial operations.
- Multilingual Interface: Support English and Amharic user experiences where implemented.
